DATA SOURCE NOTE - v5
=====================

1) SNU Korean vocabulary / TOPIK 1 collocations
The Korean-Mongolian data under data/snu and data/topik1 was copied from the user's supplied korean-quiz source data for game use.

2) Preliminary vocabulary 40
Path: data/preliminary/preliminary40.json
The 40 preliminary items were selected from words repeatedly used in the user's supplied Hangul learning PPT materials covering basic consonants, double vowels, double consonants, batchim/liaison, and review/classroom expressions.
The Mongolian meanings were checked against the Korean-Mongolian vocabulary pairs already present in the supplied SNU Korean data.
The selection intentionally focuses on basic verbs and nouns suitable for learners who are still studying Hangul consonants and vowels.

3) Quiz safety
For Mongolian -> Korean questions, the game engine automatically removes items that could create multiple Korean answers from an identical Mongolian meaning.
